## Development Plan

Based on the requirements in `doc/task9-reverse.txt`:

1. **Bidirectional Conversion Architecture (Direct & Reverse)**:
   - **Forward Mapping (Image → HSL)**: Converts pixel RGB colors $(r, g, b) \to (h, s, l)$ and maps them to circular island coordinates $(\theta = 2\pi h, r = s \cdot R_{\max})$.
   - **Reverse Mapping (HSL Diagram / Heightmap → Image)**: Reads the HSL canvas or circular heightmap, derives $(h, s, l)$ from polar coordinates and luminance/mass, samples $N$ pixels proportionally, and synthesizes a spatially coherent image in the preview canvas.
   - **Stable Non-Destructive GUI**: Both the input image canvas (`preview-canvas`) and the main HSL circular diagram (`hsl-canvas`) remain visible and update synchronously when toggling or converting back and forth.

2. **HSL Presets & Greyscale Heightmap Loader**:
   - **Example HSL Linearts**:
     - *Spiral Lineart*: Archimedean spiral sweeping across all hue angles and radial saturation levels.
     - *Radial Spokes*: 12 pure-hue radial spokes radiating from the center.
     - *Concentric Rings*: Pure saturation levels (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%).
     - *Flower Rose*: Harmonic polar curve $r(\theta) = R_{\max}(0.5 + 0.45\sin(6\theta))$.
   - **Load HSL Image (Heightmap)**: File loader that imports any image, fits it onto the circular HSL island, converts pixel luminance to heightmap values, and renders it directly as the source distribution.

3. **Roundtrip Loss & Fidelity Verification**:
   - **Metric Computation**: Computes histogram Total Variation Loss % ($\text{TV Loss} = \frac{1}{2}\sum |P_{\text{orig}} - P_{\text{roundtrip}}| \times 100\%$), Color Fidelity %, and Color Error $\Delta E$.
   - **Roundtrip Test Engine**: Executes full HSL ⇄ Image ⇄ HSL cycles and updates live stats. Geometric patterns (such as spiral lineart) remain intact across roundtrips.

4. **Monte-Carlo Spatial Relaxation**:
   - Asynchronous, frame-budgeted Monte-Carlo spatial Lloyd relaxation that iteratively swaps pixel positions to reduce spatial total variation and gradient roughness while strictly conserving 100% of the HSL color distribution.
